How the math works

For each complete day: (resting + active) − eaten = estimated deficit. The weekly predicted weight starts from the most recent prior Friday's actual weight, then subtracts the sum of complete logged deficits ÷ 3,500. Predictions remain blank if days are missing.

Calorie estimates and body weight naturally fluctuate; the chart is for exploring trends, not measuring exact fat loss.
